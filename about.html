HOW IT ALL BEGAN

Made for
Travelers

Tucked into the corner of an old European square, where cobblestones meet lantern light, Cat's Tail Café is a place made for travelers. Whether you arrive after a long day on the road or simply wandered in from the market, there is always a warm seat waiting and something good in the cup.

The first thing you will notice is the smell of fresh coffee and baked pastries. The second is the cat: a tuxedo cat who sits on the reception counter and keeps a close eye on every new arrival. Yes, you are welcome to say hello.

The idea for Cat’s Tail started with a simple combination of things I love: cats, cozy cafés, and card games.

As a fan of Genshin Impact and its TCG, I became interested in the idea of creating a café inspired by that atmosphere. I imagined a small, welcoming café on the corner of a European square, where people could enjoy a good drink, spend time with cats, and challenge their friends to a card game. This website is my own interpretation of that idea. I wanted to create something that feels warm, playful, and modern while bringing together the atmosphere of a café with the fun and competitive spirit of TCG.

Cat’s Tail is not just a café concept—it is a small idea brought to life through design and code.

BEHIND THE WEBSITE

Built to Bring an Idea to Life

Cat's Tail Café does NOT exist outside this screen. I built this website because I wanted a practice project of my own, and because I had a picture in my head that I wanted to turn into something real.

It is made with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I plan to keep growing it, including a back-end in the future.
